Why English Is Becoming the New Language of Opportunity in Morocco

25 Avril 2024

In recent years, Morocco has witnessed a noticeable shift in language trends. While French has long held a dominant position in education, administration, and business, English is increasingly seen as the language of global connection, innovation, and opportunity.

1. A Changing Linguistic Landscape

For decades, Morocco's education system placed a strong emphasis on French. However, globalization and the internet have introduced Moroccan youth to English through music, movies, social media, and international education.

In a 2023 statement, Morocco's Ministry of Education announced that English will be introduced earlier in public schools, starting as early as middle school. This reflects a national strategy to prepare Moroccan students for a more globalized world.

"English is the language of science, technology, and international business. It's time we gave Moroccan students the tools to succeed on a global stage," Ministry of Education spokesperson

2. English in Higher Education and Business

More Moroccan universities are now offering programs in English, especially in fields like engineering, IT, and international relations. Prestigious institutions such as Al Akhawayn University and UM6P are attracting students eager to study in English.

The business world is also evolving. Companies that work with international clients, especially in tourism, customer service, and tech outsourcing, often list English fluency as a top requirement.

3. Real Stories of Moroccan Success with English

Imane's Story

A 23-year-old from Settat studied English Literature at university. After graduation, she began teaching English online to international students. She now works remotely, earning a steady income and connecting with people from across the world.

Youssef's Story

A self-taught English speaker from Marrakech started a YouTube channel where he shares tech reviews in English. His channel grew rapidly, gaining followers from around the globe, and he was eventually invited to collaborate with international brands.

4. How to Start Learning English in Morocco

Luckily, learning English in Morocco has never been easier. Here are some popular and accessible options:

  • Language centers like AMIDEAST, British Council, and private academies offer structured programs
  • Online platforms like YouTube, Duolingo, and Coursera make learning flexible and affordable
  • English-speaking clubs exist in many cities where learners can practice in a fun, supportive environment
  • Social media pages like @english_academy_ma (Instagram) provide bite-sized lessons

5. Conclusion: A Language for the Future

English is more than just a foreign language in Morocco now—it's a tool for global connection, self-growth, and opportunity. As more institutions, educators, and learners embrace it, English is becoming an essential part of Morocco's journey toward innovation and international success.

Whether you're a student, a parent, or a professional, learning English today could be one of the smartest investments in your future.

Top 5 Challenges Moroccan Learners Face with English

20 Mars 2024

Learning English in Morocco can be a rewarding journey, but it doesn't come without its challenges. Many Moroccan learners, whether students or professionals, face similar obstacles when trying to master the language. The good news? With the right strategies, these challenges can be overcome.

1. Pronunciation Problems

Challenge: Certain English sounds, like "th" (as in "think" or "this"), don't exist in Arabic or French, which can make pronunciation tricky. Many Moroccan learners tend to substitute "th" with "s" or "z."

Solution:

  • Practice minimal pairs (e.g., "think" vs. "sink") daily
  • Watch YouTube pronunciation tutorials
  • Record yourself speaking and compare it to native speakers

2. Thinking in Arabic or French

Challenge: When trying to speak English, many Moroccans first think in Arabic or French, then translate. This slows down speech and leads to awkward sentences.

Solution:

  • Practice thinking directly in English by describing your surroundings or your day in simple English sentences
  • Start with short phrases like "I am walking" or "The weather is nice"

Every English learner faces difficulties, no matter where they are from. In Morocco, with its multilingual environment, learners have the advantage of already managing multiple languages - and this skill can be a real asset when learning English!
